# M3 PX4 SITL: external-vision integration (2026-10-27)

**Simulation only.** PX4 v1.17.0 SITL with Gazebo Harmonic, on the Jetson (jatom1). The "VIO" is
a fake: Gazebo ground truth plus noise, drift, latency and injected faults (`x650_sim/fake_vio`).
These numbers measure the estimator adapter, the PX4 bridge, timing and EKF2 configuration.
They say nothing about OKVIS2-X or any real estimator.

- Code: working tree on top of `886529e` (uncommitted when run). PX4 `d6f12ad` (v1.17.0),
  `px4_msgs` `86d8239`, Micro-XRCE-DDS-Agent v2.4.3, gz-sim 8.15.0. Decision 0004.
- Recordings: `data/sim/<run>/bag` (MCAP, about 10 MB each, not committed). JSON reports are in
  `reports/2026-10-27_m3_sitl/`.
- Reproduce: `scripts/setup_px4_sitl.sh`, `colcon build`, then
  `ros2 run x650_sim x650_sim_flight_test --out data/sim/m3_faults`.

## Setup

- Model `x500_vision`, world `default`, headless. GPS and magnetometer are disabled, so vision is
  the only position and heading source (`EKF2_EV_CTRL=15`, `EKF2_HGT_REF=3`).
- Fake VIO at 25 Hz (Gazebo odometry is 50 Hz, decimated). Estimator heading offset is 0.6 rad,
  latency 25 ms, white noise 3 mm, drift random walk 2 mm/sqrt(s).
- Data path: fake VIO -> `estimator_adapter` -> `/vio/odometry` -> `px4_bridge` ->
  `/fmu/in/vehicle_visual_odometry` -> EKF2.
- Mission: offboard takeoff to 2.5 m, a 3 m square with yaw following each leg, then land. The
  vehicle holds 2 s at each waypoint.
- Faults by waypoint:
  - 1: `jump 0.5` (estimator-world x shift, like a loop closure).
  - 2: `dropout 1.0` (estimator silent for 1 s).
  - 3: `reset` (new estimator world).
  - 4: `latency 0.15 1.0` (150 ms late for 1 s, above the bridge's 100 ms max age).

- **Jump step**: largest step not explained by the reported velocity within 1.5 s of a jump or

## M3 exit criteria

- **Correct axes.** With no alignment, PX4's estimate matches the odometry we send to about 4 cm
  and 1.5 deg (p95). PX4 reported heading 0.600 rad for the 0.6 rad estimator offset; that is the
  FRD sign flip of z-up yaw (decision 0002).
- **Correct timing.** Changing vision latency from 0 to 80 ms left PX4's lag (-10 ms) and accuracy
  (3.4 cm) unchanged. So EKF2 uses our `timestamp_sample` as acquisition time and compensates the
  delay, with `EKF2_EV_DELAY=0`. The remaining -10 to -20 ms is inside PX4 SITL: PX4's own IMU
  stamps are off by 16-18 ms against Gazebo, because the Gazebo bridge stamps IMU on arrival.
  Wall-clock timesync adds about 5 ms more, so sim time is the SITL default (decision 0004).
- **Stale data.** 25-29 late samples were rejected by the bridge, and none older than 100 ms was
  sent. EKF2 stopped vision fusion during each outage and resumed within about 0.7 s.
- **Global corrections don't jump local odometry.** For the 0.5 m jump and the estimator reset,
  the largest step was 1.4-3.5 cm in `/vio/odometry` and 0.2 cm in PX4's estimate. The adapter
  absorbed exactly 2 jumps (`pose_revision` 2) and counted 1 reset.

## Findings and fixes along the way

1. **Dropout handling bug (fixed; decision 0003 amended).** The adapter first treated any gap over
   0.5 s as a jump. After the 1 s dropout it threw away 0.84 m of real motion, and PX4 then reset
   its position by 0.94 m when vision returned. ATE was 1.68 m. Short gaps are now judged by a
   velocity-consistency test whose tolerance grows with the gap. There are regression tests in
   `test_odometry_splitter.cpp`.
2. **Reset count published ahead of its data (fake VIO; fixed).** The adapter counted one reset as
   2 jumps. A real estimator wrapper must publish its reset counter in step with the matching
   odometry.
3. **PX4 behavior to plan around (M7).** When vision returns after an outage of about 1 s, PX4
   corrects IMU-only drift with a 16-18 cm position step.
4. **Platform issue (worked around after these runs).** This Jetson's GPU did not initialize (`nvgpu: ACR bootstrap failed`, firmware
   load error at boot), so these runs used Gazebo without rendering. Reloading the `nvgpu` module
   later fixed it for the running boot (CUDA and Gazebo camera rendering verified); see the `gpu`
   entry in `config/dependencies.jetson.lock.yaml`.

## Not covered

- Camera-based VIO in simulation (the GPU was unavailable during these runs).
- Flight-controller hardware and its clock.
- Wind and aggressive flight.
- Long outages beyond 1 s.
